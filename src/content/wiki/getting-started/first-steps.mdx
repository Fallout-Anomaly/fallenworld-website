## What Fallen World expects from you

Fallen World is designed as a survival-focused Fallout 4 experience. Do not treat the opening hours like a normal vanilla playthrough. Supplies matter, injuries matter, weather matters, and some systems intentionally make bad preparation expensive.

<Admonition type="important" title="Alpha saves">
Fallen World is still evolving. Read the current release notes before committing to a long save and assume major updates can require a fresh game unless the release notes explicitly say otherwise.
</Admonition>

## Before you start playing

1. Finish the installation and update process completely before launching Fallout 4.
2. Do not add extra mods until you have confirmed the base Fallen World install works.
3. Launch through the supported Fallen World entry point rather than directly through Fallout 4.
4. Let startup systems finish before immediately changing menus or forcing a save.
5. Confirm the HUD, MCM, controls, and core survival systems appear normally.

## Your first hour

Focus on staying functional instead of collecting everything you see.

- Carry food, clean water, basic healing, and ammunition you actually use.
- Treat injuries instead of ignoring them until combat becomes unmanageable.
- Avoid unnecessary fights when your equipment is poor.
- Learn what the HUD is telling you before changing interface settings.
- Keep enough resources available for travel, medical treatment, and equipment upkeep.

## Learn the systems in this order

### 1. Survival and injuries

Understand what is hurting you and what kind of treatment fixes it. Fallen World separates several survival problems that vanilla Fallout 4 lets you ignore.

### 2. Combat and ballistics

Weapons are not balanced around vanilla assumptions. Projectile travel, armor interaction, ammunition, and weapon condition can matter.

### 3. Crafting and upgrades

Do not spend every component immediately. Workbench progression and equipment upgrades are part of the longer survival loop.

### 4. Radiation and weather

Bad weather is not just visual. Learn how radiation exposure, storms, shelter, and protective equipment interact before wandering far from safety.

## If something looks broken

Do not immediately reinstall everything.

1. Check the [Troubleshooting section](/wiki?category=troubleshooting).
2. Check the [Performance section](/wiki/performance/performance-basics) for freezes, low FPS, or load-time problems.
3. Confirm you are on the supported Fallen World build.
4. Remove any unapproved additions and test the base install.
5. If the problem is not documented, use **Report a Problem** at the bottom of the relevant wiki page.

## Avoid these beginner mistakes

- Installing extra mods before confirming the base setup works.
- Treating every combat encounter as mandatory.
- Ignoring injuries and radiation until they stack into a larger problem.
- Changing many performance settings at once, which makes troubleshooting harder.
- Following old guides without checking the Fallen World version badge.
