## What this guide is for

This is a short example of the kind of practical player guide the community can submit. It is intentionally simple: get through the opening days without wasting resources or turning a small problem into a run-ending one.

## Before leaving a safe area

Check that you have:

- water
- food
- basic healing supplies
- ammunition for the weapon you are actually carrying
- enough free carry weight to bring useful loot home
- a plan for where you are going

## During travel

Do not treat every landmark as a mandatory fight. If you are hurt, low on supplies, or carrying valuable equipment, walking away is often the correct decision.

Watch for:

- worsening weather
- radiation exposure
- injury penalties
- ammunition running low
- routes that leave you with no safe retreat

## After a fight

Before moving on:

1. Check injuries.
2. Reload and count remaining ammunition.
3. Take only loot that supports your current goals.
4. Decide whether continuing is worth the supplies you just spent.

## When to head home

Return to safety before you are completely out of options. A successful run that ends early is better than losing everything because you pushed one location too far.
